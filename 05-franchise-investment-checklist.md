# Franchise Investment Checklist

Before investing in a franchise business in Gurgaon, use a basic due-diligence checklist.

## Financial Checklist

- [ ] Franchise fee verified
- [ ] Total setup cost calculated
- [ ] Rental deposit estimated
- [ ] Equipment cost checked
- [ ] Inventory requirement understood
- [ ] Staff cost calculated
- [ ] Working capital available
- [ ] Royalty structure understood
- [ ] Marketing fees checked

## Location Checklist

- [ ] Target customers identified
- [ ] Local competition researched
- [ ] Footfall studied
- [ ] Accessibility checked
- [ ] Parking considered
- [ ] Rent compared with expected sales

## Franchisor Checklist

- [ ] Existing outlets researched
- [ ] Franchisees contacted
- [ ] Training details verified
- [ ] Supply arrangements understood
- [ ] Marketing support evaluated
- [ ] Franchise agreement reviewed

## Final Decision

Do not make an investment decision only on projected ROI or sales figures provided during a franchise presentation.

Compare the assumptions with actual operating conditions and conduct independent financial and legal due diligence before signing an agreement.
